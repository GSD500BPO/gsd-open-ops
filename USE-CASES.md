# Use cases

Eight ways teams use GSD Open Ops, each written as a short story with steps, the files it uses and what to measure. Personas are generic; the playbooks are the ones we run.

| 2 | [A roofing and restoration company pivots its list when the season changes](docs/use-cases/storm-season-pivot.md) | Roofing, solar, storm restoration | `96-step-journey.json`, `scenarios/storm_restoration.json` |
| 3 | [100 appointment-setter applicants → a top 10 worth interviewing](docs/use-cases/recruiting-100-to-top-10.md) | BPOs, call centers, in-house desks | `recruiting/` |

| 5 | [Door-to-door data into the CRM, so every knock keeps paying](docs/use-cases/door-to-door-data-to-crm.md) | Canvassing teams | `crm/cadences.md`, `playbooks/` |
| 6 | [A new appointment setter on the phones in a week, graduated in two](docs/use-cases/train-a-setter-in-a-week.md) | Team leads, trainers | `training/` |
| 7 | [The same playbook on Zoho CRM, GoHighLevel or HubSpot](docs/use-cases/crm-agnostic-setup.md) | Agencies, CRM admins | `crm/README.md`, `96-step-journey.csv` |
