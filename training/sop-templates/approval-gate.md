# The approval gate: nothing goes out without an explicit "send"

Applies to email, SMS, WhatsApp, campaigns, social posts, ad changes and loading contacts into live systems.

1. Drafts and previews are free. Sending is not.
2. The approval must name **that** send ("send the Tuesday reminder to the 40 webinar registrants"). Choosing a plan ("let's go with option A") is not approval.
3. Bulk sends (more than a handful of recipients) need the pre-send checklist **and** a second explicit "go" on the final list.
4. Pre-send checklist: right brand and sender line · opted-out and recently-contacted people removed · merge fields render (send a test to yourself) · links work · quiet hours respected (8 a.m. – 8 p.m. in the recipient's time zone) · STOP/unsubscribe instructions present.
5. Every automated sender has a daily cap per recipient, writes its "sent" marker **before** sending, and has a kill switch.
6. STOP is permanent for that channel. Nobody gets re-enrolled automatically.
