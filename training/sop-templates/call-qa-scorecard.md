# Call QA scorecard (100 points, B2B appointment setting)

| Item | Points | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| Fast open | 10 | Name, company and reason in under 15 seconds; speaks in the first 2 seconds |
| Clear reason | 10 | One sentence on why they called and why it matters to this prospect |
| Discovery | 15 | 3-5 open questions, then listens |
| **Meeting ask** | **20** | Asks for a meeting with a specific day AND time |
| Next step confirmed | 10 | Repeats date/time in the prospect's time zone, confirms email/channel and the day-before confirmation |
| Lead source kept secret* | 10 | Never names an ad platform, list or data source |
| No unproven claims* | 10 | No revenue, "X appointments a week" or client-logo claims without proof |
| Respects STOP* | 5 | "Stop/remove me" → apologizes, ends the call, marks DNC before the next dial |
| Professional* | 5 | No profanity, no jokes about any market, never reads PINs/cards/emails aloud |
| Language mirror | 5 | Matches the prospect's language and formality (usted/tú) |

\* Compliance items: when failed, quote the exact words from the recording.

**Good** ≥ 80 · **Bad** 60-79 (coach this week) · **Ugly** < 60 or any compliance fail (escalate the same day).
