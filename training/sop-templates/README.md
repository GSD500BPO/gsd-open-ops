# SOP templates

Short, copyable operating procedures for a small sales floor.

| File | What it is | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| [`daily-routine-and-eod.md`](daily-routine-and-eod.md) | The setter's day and the end-of-day report | Setters, ops coordinator |
| [`call-qa-scorecard.md`](call-qa-scorecard.md) | 100-point call scorecard: Good ≥ 80 · Bad 60-79 · Ugly < 60 or any compliance fail | QA supervisor, trainer |
| [`approval-gate.md`](approval-gate.md) | Nothing goes out without an explicit "send": pre-send checklist, caps, kill switch, STOP is permanent | Everyone who sends email/SMS/WhatsApp |

## How to use
Paste them into your wiki or LMS, put your own names in the role column, and review them in the first week of training. The QA scorecard works as a spreadsheet: one row per graded call.

## How to adapt
Keep the compliance items (lead source, unproven claims, STOP, professionalism) as automatic fails. Change point weights only after grading 25+ calls with the original.
