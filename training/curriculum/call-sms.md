# Step 8: SMS follow-up

**Send only from the company's registered texting line.** The other company line is for calls only. Use only approved templates; anything new goes to your manager for review first.

## After a voicemail

> "Hi {first name}, {your name} from GSD 500 BPO. Just left you a voicemail about booking more bilingual appointments for {company}. Open to a 15-min call this week? Reply STOP to opt out."

## After a booked meeting

> "Thanks {first name}! Confirmed: {day} {time} {TZ}, invite sent to {email}. Please be at a computer so you can see the calendars. {your name}, GSD 500 BPO. Reply STOP to opt out."

## After "send me info"

> "Hi {first name}, as promised, info sent to {email} (check spam). When's a good 15 min to walk you through it, {option A} or {option B}? Reply STOP to opt out."

## STOP means stop

If anyone replies STOP, stop, don't call or remove:

- **Do not** reply with a pitch.
- Mark DNC / opt-out in the phone system and in Zoho the same day.
- Stop all contact.

## Timing

- Marketing texts only between **8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in the lead's time zone**.
- **Bulk sends** (more than a handful of numbers) are never yours to fire alone. They need the full pre-send checklist in **QA scorecard & pre-send checks** and an explicit second "go" from your manager.
