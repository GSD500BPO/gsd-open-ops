## How the Academy works

1. **Modules.** Each module has a reading page (this site) and, where it helps, embedded videos. Read it fully before the quiz.
2. **Quiz.** Every module ends with a short assessment. Passing score is **70–80%** depending on the module. You can retake it.
3. **Order matters.** Orientation first, then the call modules, then CRM and QA, then the 4-week Zoho video curriculum.
4. **3-3-3 graduation.** Book your first real calls in three phases:
   - **Phase 1 — WITH your manager:** 3 calls where your manager leads and you listen.
   - **Phase 2 — OBSERVED:** 3 calls where you lead and your manager listens and scores.
   - **Phase 3 — ALONE:** 3 calls on your own, reviewed afterwards with the QA scorecard.
   After Phase 3 you are **Graduated** and get your own pipeline.

## The golden rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Nothing goes out without your manager's explicit approval.** Emails, SMS, WhatsApp, campaigns, posts, ads, loading contacts into live systems. Drafts and previews are free; sending is not.
2. **Choosing a plan is not approval to send.** Send a review copy first and wait for an explicit "send" / "approved" that names that send.
3. **Texts go only from the company's registered 10DLC texting line.** The other line is calls-only.
4. **Before any bulk SMS:** check the full message history for every number, remove anyone who ever said STOP, remove anyone texted in the last 14 days, confirm the brand, show the final list and wait for a second explicit "go".
5. **Never tell a client or dealer where leads come from.** Say "our hybrid lead engine" and stop there.
6. **Never touch live ads** unless your manager tells you to in writing, in that same message.
7. **Never create parallel CRM objects** (rules, templates, fields, views). Fix the object your manager is looking at.
8. **Verify before reporting.** Check the live state yourself. Anything you could not check is reported as **UNVERIFIED**.
9. **STOP means stop.** Anyone who says stop, remove me or do not call is marked do-not-contact the same minute.

**Also:** no social posts until your manager approves the visuals; marketing texts only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in the lead's time zone.

## When something goes wrong

Say it first, in plain words, then explain what is running now. Hiding a mistake is worse than the mistake.
