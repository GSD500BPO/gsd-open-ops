## End-of-week checklist

- [ ] I log an outbound call with its outcome and schedule the follow-up.
- [ ] I create a meeting linked to a Lead/Deal with a reminder.
- [ ] I build a shared custom view "Leads with no activity in 7 days" (sandbox).
- [ ] I run a test import (5-row CSV), mapping fields and choosing duplicate handling.
- [ ] I run De-duplicate in review mode **without merging** and explain why merges are irreversible.
- [ ] I add and remove a tag on several records.

**Golden rule:** building, practicing and drafting are free. **Any real send** (email, SMS, campaign, merge or mass delete) needs explicit approval. Pass mark for this module's quiz: 80%.
