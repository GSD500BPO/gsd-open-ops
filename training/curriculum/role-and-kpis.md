# Your Role, Schedule and KPIs

**Role:** Appointment Setter + Scrum Master + Campaign QA Supervisor. Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. Bogotá time (COT), remote. You report to your manager. Compensation: base + commissions (details in your contract).

> **Rule number one:** nothing goes out without your manager's explicit approval. Prepare drafts freely; the send waits for a "send" or "approved" that names that send.

## Duties

### 1. Appointment setting — priority #1
- **Minimum 4 qualified booked appointments per week. That is a floor, not a ceiling.** Target is 6+.
- An appointment **counts** only when all four are true:
  1. Date and time confirmed by the prospect, repeated aloud in **their** time zone.
  2. Logged in Zoho: status updated, full note, event or meeting created.
  3. Handoff complete (checklist below).
  4. Reconfirmed the day before (call or approved message).
- **5-minute rule:** a new lead gets its first call within 5 minutes, during business hours.
- **3 touches in 48 h:** call → approved text from the registered texting line → second call + CRM email the next business day. Then the lead goes "stale" and into the weekly revive cycle.
- **Handoff checklist (in the lead note):** verified name and phone · address · date and time · who will be present · main problem · language · owner or renter · special notes.
- **Never tell a client or dealer where leads come from.** Say "our hybrid lead engine" and stop there.

### 2. Scrum Master (Trello)
Run the 8:00 standup (15 min max), keep every card with an owner, honest stars, a date and a next action. Max 2 cards per person in "Doing". Apply the 2-hour rule. See the SCRUM module.

### 3. Campaign QA
Every active campaign gets QA: recorded calls (min. 5 a day), CRM data, email before send, SMS before send, automations daily. See the QA module.

### 4. CRM hygiene
Every call is logged. If it is not in Zoho, it did not happen. See the CRM module.
