# Step 2: Gatekeepers & call screeners

## Automated call screener ("say your name and reason")

One line, under 10 seconds:

> "**{Your name}, GSD 500 BPO, about bilingual appointment setting for water-treatment dealers.**"

A vague reason ("just testing the waters", only your name, only a brand) gets you rejected.

## Receptionist

> "Hi, this is {your name} with GSD 500 BPO. It's in regards to **bilingual lead generation and an appointment-setting partnership.** Who handles marketing or appointment setting there? Is that {owner name}?"

## If the decision maker isn't available, collect three things

1. "**Do you have bilingual appointment setters making calls for you today? Is that in-house or outsourced, so I know what to offer?**"
2. "**What's the best email to reach {owner}, so I can be proactive?**"
3. "**And who may I say I spoke to?**"

Then book the callback:

> "When is {owner} usually at the desk, morning or afternoon? I'll call back then."

## Calling back on an agreement? Name the agreement

> "{Owner} asked me to call him at **10 AM today**, and I sent him the info by email yesterday. Could you put me through?"

"He told me to call back" on its own gets you sent to voicemail. A specific time and a specific promise get you through.
