# Zoho 6: Campaigns & email marketing

**Week 3 of the 4-week Zoho track.** One 60-minute block a day: 35–45 min of video or reading, 15–20 min of practice in the **sandbox / test account** (never on real client records without approval).

> Tip: watch English videos with auto-translated Spanish captions (YouTube → Settings → Subtitles → Auto-translate → Spanish). Long videos: 1.25x speed.

## End-of-week checklist

- [ ] Before editing a record, I check which rules, blueprint or cadence affect it.
- [ ] I enrol and unenrol a test lead in a cadence (sandbox).
- [ ] I can explain how a CRM record reaches a Campaigns list (sync).
- [ ] I QA a campaign draft: right list/segment, right language, unsubscribe link, sender, test sent **only to internal addresses**.
- [ ] I read a campaign report: opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes and spam complaints.

---

**Golden rule:** building, practicing and drafting are free. **Any real send** (email, SMS, campaign, merge or mass delete) needs explicit approval. Pass mark for this module's quiz: 80%.
