# Hard rules on every call

**We Get Sh\*t Done.** That's the brand. On the phone we say **"GSD 500 BPO"** and never spell out the tagline. Saying it on a voicemail or to a front desk costs credibility.

Read these before every shift. Breaking one of them is an automatic **Ugly** on the QA scorecard.

## 1. Lead source stays inside GSD

- Never tell a dealer, contractor or client where our leads or prospects come from. Say **"our hybrid lead engine"** and stop there.
- If they push: *"It's a mix of field work and inbound requests. The playbook is what you're paying us for."*
- If they ask how you found *them*: *"I came across your company while researching dealers in your area."*
- Your manager keeps the account-specific list of words that are off-limits. Learn it before your first dial.

## 2. STOP and do-not-call

If anyone says "stop", "remove me", "don't call", "add us to your do-not-call list" or "not interested, don't contact me":

> **"Understood. I'll remove you right now. Sorry for the interruption, have a good day."**

- Hang up and mark DNC in the phone system and in Zoho **before your next dial**.
- No "but", no second pitch.

## 3. No numbers we can't prove

- Never quote revenue figures, "X appointments a week", "Fortune 500" clients or "a successful exit".
- Unproven claims are what turned a past call hostile.
- Offer proof on the screen instead: *"I'd rather show you than tell you."*

## 4. No pretext and no robocalls

- Never say "we're sponsored" when we aren't.
- Never pitch press-1 prerecorded dialing.

## 5. Recorded line = professional line

- No flirting, no profanity, no jokes about any market or community.
- Never read PINs, card numbers or a lead's email or address out loud.
- Never name one prospect to another.

## 6. Speak in the first 2 seconds

Dead air kills live connects. Test your headset before the first dial so the prospect never hears an echo.

## 7. Call in *their* business hours

Not ours. Check the prospect's time zone before you dial. Marketing texts go out only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in the lead's time zone.
