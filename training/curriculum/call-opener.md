# Step 1: The opener (cold B2B)

**Who you're calling:** a water-treatment dealer or a home-service contractor.
**Goal:** get permission for 30 seconds, then ask one question.

## The script

> "Hi, is this **{first name}**? ... {First name}, this is **{your name}** with **GSD 500 BPO**. I'm calling from our team in Bogotá, Colombia. We're a bilingual appointment-setting and back-office team for water-treatment and home-service companies.
>
> I'm not calling to sell you anything today. I'm calling to **compete for your business the good old-fashioned way.** Do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I called, and you tell me if it's worth a meeting?"

If they say yes:

> "We help dealers book more in-home appointments, especially in the **Latino homeowner market**. We bring bilingual setters, follow-up automation and our own lead engine. Quick question: **are you still selling {brand} systems in {city}?** / **Are you booking as many appointments as your reps can run?**"

## Why it works

- "Compete for your business the good old-fashioned way" is honest and disarming. On real calls it got owners to say "you read my mind".
- Asking if they still sell a specific brand shows you did your homework.
- "Are you interested in booking more appointments in the Latino market?" is a question almost every owner answers "well, of course".

## The "Colombia" moment

Some prospects hear "Colombia" and check out. Say it **once, confidently**, then go straight to the question. Don't apologize for it. It's an advantage: same time zone, fully bilingual.

## Checklist

- [ ] Name, company and reason in under 15 seconds
- [ ] Asked for permission (30 seconds)
- [ ] One question, then silence
