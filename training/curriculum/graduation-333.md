# The 3-3-3 graduation program

You've read the modules and passed the quizzes. Now you prove it on **live calls**. The portal tracks your progress through four stages: **Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3 → Graduated**.

| Phase | Calls | Who drives | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: WITH your manager** | 3 | Your manager | You sit in (or co-pilot) while your manager runs the call. You take notes on the opener, discovery and close. |
| **Phase 2: OBSERVED** | 3 | You | You run the call; your manager listens live (or reviews the recording the same day) and scores it with the QA scorecard. |
| **Phase 3: ALONE** | 3 | You | You run the call on your own. Each call is scored afterwards from the recording. |
| **Graduated** | — | You | You own your pipeline: your leads board, your report card, your weekly KPIs. |

## What counts as a call

- A **live conversation** with a real prospect (not a voicemail, not a gatekeeper-only call).
- Logged in Zoho with a complete note **within 10 minutes**.
- Scored with the 100-point scorecard (see **QA scorecard & pre-send checks**).

## To move to the next phase

- All 3 calls of the phase are logged and scored.
- Average score **85+ (Good)** on Phase 2 and Phase 3 calls.
- **Zero critical fails.** One critical fail (lead source revealed, unauthorized pricing, ignoring STOP, false promise, disrespect, booking without confirming time and address) resets that phase.
- Your manager signs off in the portal.

## Graduation day

- Your manager confirms Phase 3 in the portal, your status changes to **Graduated** and you get your own lead pipeline.
- From then on your **weekly report card** (training, calls, appointments, show rate, QA) is what we coach from.

## Tips that get people through fast

1. Re-read **Step 5: Booking** and **Step 6: Objections** the night before Phase 2.
2. Record yourself on a practice call and score it with the scorecard before your manager does.
3. Speak in the first 2 seconds. Use the prospect's name three times. Always leave with a date, a time, an email and a channel.
