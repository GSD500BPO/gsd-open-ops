# Step 6: The top 10 objections

Real wording from real calls. **Acknowledge → empathize → redirect.** Never argue.

### 1. "How did you get my number?"
> "Fair question. You're listed as an authorized dealer on {brand}'s public dealer locator. That's where I found you."

Be specific and true. It keeps the call alive.

### 2. "I don't have a lot of time" / "Call me later" / "I'm with a client"
> "No problem, I'll be quick to respect your time. When's better, **tonight after 8 or tomorrow morning?** And is {email} still the right email for the info?"

Get a time and confirm the email. "Ok, sure" with no time is a lost lead.

### 3. "Not interested" / "I'm not the right person"
> "Understood. Out of curiosity, who handles appointment setting for you today?"

The "wrong person" is sometimes a middleman who can open several doors. If they repeat "not interested" or say stop, go to **Hard rules #2** and exit politely.

### 4. "We already have setters" / "We already have an assistant in Colombia"
> "Great, then you know the model works. Would you mind if I sent you **a few quality leads in your area** to test, no strings? If they close, we talk."

Or pivot to what they don't have: "Then the setters aren't the gap. Is it the CRM follow-up?"

### 5. "How much does it cost?"
> "It depends on the setup. Setters, CRM and leads are priced by volume. I'll show you the exact options on screen. Does {time} work?"

Use the anchor number from your manager's current rate card, never one you made up. Dodging the question entirely loses the meeting.

### 6. "We don't target the Hispanic market / not a fit"
> "It doesn't have to be the Latino market. Are you doing any door to door? Would you be open to testing a few leads on a split if they close?"

### 7. "We already have a CRM"
> "Perfect, we work with what you have. Let me show you the full menu, and you tell me what works for you."

### 8. "Is this a sales call?"
> "Yes, honestly. I'm competing for your business. Is now bad, or can I have 30 seconds?"

If they ask to be put on DNC, go to **Hard rules #2** immediately.

### 9. "Prove it" / "What happens if it doesn't work?"
> "You shouldn't believe me on a phone call. Let me show you real calendars and real recordings on screen, and we can start with **test leads** so the risk is on us. Can we do that in 45 minutes, or tomorrow?"

Offering proof on screen is what books the meeting. Don't answer with revenue claims.

### 10. "What do the numbers look like? How many leads a week?"
> "We start slow on purpose: a few test leads in your territory, you tell us the quality, then we scale. I won't promise a number I haven't seen in your market."

### Bonus: "I don't speak Spanish. Does that help or hurt?"
> "No problem. Our setters handle the Spanish, and your appointment is set with the information you need."

### Bonus: "Where are you calling from? Mexico?"
> "From Bogotá, Colombia. It's an advantage: same time zone, bilingual team, outsourcing costs. Can I show you how we work in 15 minutes?"
