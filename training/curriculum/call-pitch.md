# Step 4: The value pitch (GSD 500 BPO services)

Pick the **1 or 2** services that match what you heard in discovery. **Never list all of them.** Buzzword lists lose calls.

| What they said | What you pitch |
|---|---|
| "We can't keep up" / "I need someone more intense on appointments" | **Appointment setting:** bilingual setters who call every lead fast and keep calling. "You choose them, we train them, we handle the overhead." Pricing comes from your manager's current rate card; never improvise a number. |
| "Everything is on paper" / "leads go cold" | **CRM automation** (GoHighLevel or Zoho): speed-to-lead, reminders, follow-up journeys. "Just because they don't buy today doesn't mean they don't buy in three months." |
| "We need more leads" | **Our hybrid lead engine:** homeowners who asked to know what's in their water. Offer **a few test leads** first, then the terms your manager approves. |
| "Our site is weak / we're not showing up" | **Websites and local SEO** pages in English and Spanish. |
| "We don't know who to call" | **Databases and research:** verified lists of businesses or new homeowners in their territory, cleaned and loaded into their CRM. |
| "Not sure where we're leaking" | **Free audit / consultation:** "I can offer a free audit to show you where something might need tightening." |
| "What you're offering is what I already do myself" | "Exactly, and you're doing it all yourself. This is the part you delegate so you can sell." |

## Differentiators you can say (because they're true)

- "We're in Bogotá, so we're on your time zone and fully bilingual."
- "**One partner per territory.**" (Confirm with your manager that the policy is current.)
- Risk reversal: "**I'll put some skin in the game.**" Use only the exact terms your manager has approved.

## Never

- Promise results, revenue or appointment counts.
- Say where leads come from (see **Hard rules**).
