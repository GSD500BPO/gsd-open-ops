# Step 3: Discovery questions (ask, then shut up)

Ask **3 to 5** of these, not all of them. Halfway through, check in:

> "**Am I talking too fast, or is everything okay so far?**"

## The question bank

1. "How are you getting appointments today: your own ads, referrals, door to door, a lead vendor?"
2. "**Where does your lead data live? Do you have a CRM?**" (A common answer is "everything is on paper." That's a CRM-automation opening.)
3. "How fast does a new lead get a call today? Who makes it?"
4. "What happens to the leads that don't buy on the first visit?" (This sets up the follow-up pitch.)
5. "Do you have anyone who can run appointments in Spanish?"
6. "How many appointments a week can your reps actually run? What's the ceiling?"
7. "**Is there anything else you do with that data once you have it in-house?**" (This question has uncovered entire extra business lines, like roofing or solar.)
8. "What does a good appointment cost you today?"
9. "If we worked together, how would you want to verify the leads?"

## Rules

- Open questions. Pause. Listen. Take notes in the CRM while they talk.
- Use their name. Match their pace.
- Don't pitch during discovery. You'll pitch exactly what they told you they need in Step 4.
