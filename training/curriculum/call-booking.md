# Step 5: Booking the appointment

**Goal:** a specific time on the calendar.

## Two options, both specific

> "The easiest way is for me to show you on screen. No fluff, no long deck, straight to results, and you decide from there with no obligation.
> I have **tomorrow at 11** or **Thursday at 2, your time.** Which works better?"

## When they give a time

> "**Lock that in.** What's the best email for the invite? Do you use WhatsApp for this number? I'll send a reminder and a couple of short recordings so you can see how we work."
>
> "You'll want to be in front of a computer, not on your phone, so you can see the calendars."
>
> "I'll confirm the day before. Talk to you {day} at {time}, {time zone}."

## If they stall ("I don't have a lot of time")

> "That's exactly why I'd rather do it when you're comfortable. Let's not do it now. **What about later tonight, or first thing tomorrow?**"

This exact line has locked same-day evening meetings.

## Confirmation call (30 to 60 minutes before)

> "Hi {name}, {your name} from GSD 500 BPO. Just confirming our meeting in 50 minutes. Will you be at a computer so you can see the calendars?"

## Never end a live call without

1. A **date and time** (repeated back in *their* time zone)
2. An **email** for the invite
3. A **channel** for the reminder (SMS or WhatsApp)

Calls that ended on "I'll send you an email" stalled. Every time.

## Log it

Create the meeting in Zoho, update the status and write the handoff note before you dial again.
