# Step 7: Voicemail (under 45 seconds, number twice)

## The script

> "Hi {first name}, this is {your name} with **GSD 500 BPO**, calling from Bogotá about **booking more appointments in the Latino homeowner market** for {company}.
>
> {Personal hook, e.g. "You signed up for our webinar on lead gen for water treatment" / "I saw you're a {brand} dealer in {city}."}
>
> I'd like 15 minutes to understand what's working for you and whether we can earn your business. No obligation.
>
> My number is **{{CALLBACK_NUMBER}}**. Again, **{{CALLBACK_NUMBER}}**. I'll also send you a quick text. Have a great day."

## Do

- Slow down on **your name, the company and the number**. Garbled names cost callbacks.
- Add one **personal hook** so it doesn't sound like a blast.
- Follow with the SMS from **Step 8** within 30 minutes.

## Don't

- Leave 2-minute monologues listing every service.
- Make claims or quote numbers.
- Use internal jargon or tool names.
- Forget the number.
