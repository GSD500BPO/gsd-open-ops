# Playbooks: CRM journey templates for home-service businesses

Free, importable nurture journeys for leads that didn't buy. Watch the walkthrough: [The 96-Step CRM Journey That Sells All Year](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fA0oPEkFEo4).

## [96-Step Year-Round Prospect Journey](96-step-journey/)
What to do with every lead that didn't buy: a 365-day email + SMS journey in 4 phases (water treatment → roofing/solar/storm restoration → windows & doors → HVAC & indoor air), 3 chapters per phase, with stop rules, personalization fields and a CRM map (Zoho CRM / GoHighLevel / HubSpot).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `GSD-96-Step-Year-Round-Prospect-Journey.pdf` | Read it (11 pages) |
| `96-step-journey.csv` | Import the steps into your CRM or sheet |
| `96-step-journey.json` | Same steps + negotiation series, merge fields, exit rules, for automation |

Build the exit rules first: STOP or unsubscribe ends every sequence immediately and the contact is never re-enrolled automatically; any reply pauses the journey and goes to a human.

## How to adapt it
- **Different core trade:** Phase 1 is your core product (the "Trade A" slot). Rewrite its 24 steps; keep the chapter rhythm (education → money → final push).
- **Different seasons:** reorder phases 2-4 to match when your market buys (storm season, cooling season, tax-refund season).
- **Only one trade?** Run Phase 1 four times a year with fresh angles, or stop at step 24 and use the 24-step version.
