# Cadences

## First contact (new lead)
- **3 touches in 48 hours:** call → approved text from the registered texting line → second call + CRM email the next business day.
- Each attempt is a numbered note: TA-1, TA-2, TA-3, TA-4.
- No contact after that → "stale" → weekly revive cycle.
- Every new lead carries an open follow-up task until an appointment is booked (24-hour rule).

## Follow-up cadence (no appointment yet)
- Had a conversation → next task in **3 days**.
- No conversation → next task in **5 business days**.
- Every task has a pop-up reminder. Every call and text is a numbered note.
- Supervisor audit per record: numbered notes present · next task dated and owned · stage matches the last note · required fields filled.

## Booking (water-treatment example)
1. Call the same or next day after any text reply.
2. Find the decision maker(s); book with **both** present.
3. Sell the concept (a free in-home water analysis), not the price.
4. Offer two specific slots while still on the call.
5. Convert the lead to a deal; write a standard appointment card (name, phone, address, date/time, who'll be present, main problem, language, owner/renter, notes).
6. Card + call recording go into the deal notes, the shared field calendar (block 90 minutes for the specialist) and the team's appointments channel.
7. Reconfirm the day before.

## Post-appointment
- Right after booking: a post-visit follow-up task.
- Then a new task every 2-3 days up to at least **FU-6** (reschedule, negotiation, financing...).
- Outcomes are color-coded in the weekly tracker: negotiation · lost · financing · closed.

## Long-term nurture
After a visit without a sale, or a prospect who went quiet: the [96-step year-round journey](../playbooks/96-step-journey/) (email + SMS, pivots by trade and season). Any reply pauses it and goes to a human. STOP ends it for good.

## Fields that drive automation
Set them from real conversations, not guesses: **business/lead type**, **preferred language**, **preferred channel**, **owner or renter**, **best time to call**. Clean fields in = the right message out. Lead source is stored for reporting only and is never merged into anything a customer or client sees.

## Restaurant / B2B variant (validation first)
1. Validation text ("are you open today?") before any pitch.
2. Research the business on Google Maps (phone, menu, website, socials) and copy new data into the CRM.
3. If texts and calls go unanswered, try the website contact form.
4. Only after a reply: a personalized asset (e.g. a mockup of their own menu).
5. Follow-up call the same or next day, book.
