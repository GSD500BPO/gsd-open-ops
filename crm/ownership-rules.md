# Ownership rules

These rules exist so no lead goes cold and nobody fights over credit. They only work if every touch is logged.

## Rule 1 · Speed to lead (15 minutes)
Inbound leads are assigned in rotation. The owner has **15 minutes** for first contact (5 minutes is the target). After that, the next agent in line may call, with the team lead's sign-off.

## Rule 2 · Log every first contact, immediately
Every attempt is a numbered note the moment the call ends: **TA-1** (e.g. 3 calls + voicemail + text), **TA-2**, ... Then create the next task with a due date, owner and a pop-up reminder. "I'll log it later" doesn't exist. A booked appointment gets a booking note, the call recording, and a post-visit follow-up task.

## Rule 3 · Nurture needs a dated task
Moving a deal to Nurturing means regular value messages **plus** a dated task for the agreed callback. Nurturing without a task counts as abandoned.

## Rule 4 · Every lead and deal always has one open task
Set a task every 2-3 days and update the stage after each touch. **No open task or activity = anyone may claim the record right away.** Check open activities before you claim.

## Rule 5 · Overdue 3 business days = claimable
If a task is overdue 3 business days, another rep may claim the record or a supervisor reassigns it. You only claim it if you call it **now**: call → change owner → leave a note → set the next task. On deals, whoever books the appointment that closes earns the commission.

## Rule 6 · The setter who booked it reschedules it
A missed or cancelled appointment goes back to the setter who booked it. Clear ownership stops two people calling the same homeowner.

## Rule 7 · Joint work = shared credit
When two reps agree to work a lead together (e.g. a referral handed to an English speaker, or a callback someone else picked up), the credit is shared. Write the agreement in the record.

## Exceptions that keep it human
- A prospect who asks to be called on a specific date: log it, set the task on that date, respect it.
- STOP / do-not-call: mark it before the next dial. It's permanent.
