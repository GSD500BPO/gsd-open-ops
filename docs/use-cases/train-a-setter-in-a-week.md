# Use case: a new appointment setter on the phones in a week, graduated in two

**Persona:** a team lead who just hired a bilingual setter (maybe through the recruiting bot) and has no time to train from scratch.
**Uses:** [`training/curriculum/`](../../training/curriculum/) · [`training/quizzes/`](../../training/quizzes/) · [`training/sop-templates/`](../../training/sop-templates/) · [`training/role-map.md`](../../training/role-map.md)

## The story
New setters usually learn by shadowing whoever has time, so every setter learns a different version of the job. The academy gives everyone the same rules, the same opener and the same objection answers, in their language, with a quiz that proves they read it.

## The week
| Day | Modules (EN or ES) | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome · Role & KPIs · SCRUM board · **Hard rules (100% pass)** | Knows the rules that get you fired; has a board card |
| 2 | Opener · Gatekeepers · Discovery | Role-plays the first 60 seconds with the trainer |
| 3 | Value pitch · Booking · Top objections | Books a mock appointment with both decision makers |
| 4 | Voicemail · SMS · CRM process | Logs TA-1 notes and next tasks correctly in the CRM |
| 5 | QA scorecard & pre-send checks · **3-3-3 starts** | 3 calls WITH the manager |

Week 2: 3 OBSERVED calls, then 3 calls ALONE, graded on the 100-point scorecard (Good ≥ 80). The Zoho track (8 modules) runs over 4 weeks next to live work.

## Steps for the team lead
1. Load the modules into your LMS (or share the GitHub links) and the quizzes as question banks.
2. Swap discovery/pitch/objections for your trade before day 2.
3. Grade every week-2 call with the scorecard; any compliance fail is an automatic "Ugly" and gets coached the same day.
4. Use the daily routine + EOD template from day 1, so the habit starts with the job.

## What to measure
Days to graduation (target ≤ 14, from the role map) · quiz pass rate on the first try · QA average of the cohort · first-month appointments vs. the floor (4+ qualified a week).
