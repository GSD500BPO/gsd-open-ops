# Use case: B2B outreach to local businesses (restaurants), validation first

**Persona:** an agency or BPO selling a service to local businesses (here, restaurants: menus, websites, marketing).
**Uses:** [`crm/cadences.md`](../../crm/cadences.md#restaurant--b2b-variant-validation-first) · [`training/curriculum/call-gatekeeper.md`](../../training/curriculum/call-gatekeeper.md) · [`training/curriculum/call-sms.md`](../../training/curriculum/call-sms.md)

## The story
Cold pitches to restaurant owners go nowhere: the number is wrong, the place closed, or the person answering isn't the owner. Validate first, research second, pitch last, with something made for them.

## Steps
1. **Validation text** before any pitch: "Are you open today?" A reply confirms the number and opens a conversation.
2. **Research** the business on its public map listing and website: phone, menu, website, socials. Copy new data into the CRM (business type, language, preferred channel).
3. **No answer to texts or calls?** Use the website contact form.
4. **After a reply:** a personalized asset, for example a mockup of their own menu.
5. **Follow-up call** the same or next day; book the meeting with the owner.
6. **Every touch logged**, one open task, the 3-day / 5-day cadence like any other lead.

## What to measure
Validation reply rate · owner reached rate · meetings booked per 100 businesses researched.
