# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**In short:** be respectful and welcoming, in English or Spanish. No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or sharing anyone's private information (including real candidate or customer data). Critique ideas, not people.

**Reporting:** email **security@gsd500bpo.com** with "Code of Conduct" in the subject. Reports are confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.
