# SOP: reviewing candidates (for recruiters)

**Goal:** recruiters only talk to candidates who did BOTH steps: the 1-minute English video and the prequal test.

## What happens automatically
1. Lead comes in (ad, lead form or apply page). Within ~2 minutes: WhatsApp with the 3 steps, an email with the test link, and an ATS record.
2. The candidate sends the video on WhatsApp. It is stored privately, attached to the ATS record and speaking-scored.
3. The candidate takes the test. Results reach the pipeline.
   - **Pass** (test + video + speaking): card in the recruiting channel, ATS "Qualified", interview task for the recruiter, "you passed" on WhatsApp.
   - **Fail**: ATS "Unqualified". No message to the candidate.
   - **Passed without a video**: the bot asks for the video again.
4. No video 24 h after the welcome: one reminder. Then at most two more reminders, only inside WhatsApp's free 24-hour window.
5. STOP: we never write to that candidate again.

## What the recruiter does
- Watch only the recruiting channel. Each card has: video (signed link, expires), scores, form answers, WhatsApp link, ATS link.
- B2/C1: book the interview. B1: watch the whole video first. "NOT auto-scored": watch the video and decide.
- Book the interview on WhatsApp and record it in the ATS.
- Publishing on the public talent page needs the candidate's YES; approve it in your admin view.
- Any manual override → ATS note with the reason.

## Interview checklist (15 minutes, on video)
- [ ] Same person as the intro video
- [ ] One unexpected question (no script): "Tell me about a customer who was angry with you."
- [ ] 2-minute live roleplay from the scenario file (price objection + spouse objection)
- [ ] Schedule, internet, quiet place, headset, own computer
- [ ] Availability and start date
- [ ] Decision logged in the ATS the same day
